# Model images, 3D sources and licenses

## Station Lite (first generation)

- `station-lite.png`: unmodified official product image from Yandex Support, 540 × 541 pixels.
- Source page: https://alice.yandex.ru/support/ru/station/meet/light-ring
- Original file: https://alice.yandex.ru/support/docs-assets/alice/rev/r18743130/ru/_assets/device-card/station/station-lite.png
- Retrieved: 2026-10-26. Image rights belong to Yandex; the project's MIT license does not apply to this image.
- Stored locally for display in the panel without external image requests. A visible attribution links to the source.
- Used for `Yandex Station Micro` / `station-micro` and `yandex-station-lite` device identities.
  This changes only the displayed image and name, not the hardware command profile.

## 3D models

- Lite 2: the original, unchanged STL by vanyabeat,
  https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:7349611 , CC BY-SA 4.0. See also 3d/README.md.
- Station 2, Mini 3 Pro and Mini 3: original approximate visual reconstructions in
  model-studio.js (MIT), based on the manufacturer's public product photographs.
  They are not official CAD, downloaded commercial meshes, or manufacturing drawings.

References:
- https://alice.yandex.ru/station-gen2
- https://alice.yandex.ru/station-mini-gen3-pro
- https://alice.yandex.ru/station-mini-gen3

Mini 3 has a lower 102 × 60.5 mm body and central indicator; Mini 3 Pro has a
102 × 71 mm body and perimeter light strip. Details and colours are approximate.
These 3D reconstructions do not bundle product photographs or use them as textures. All models use lightweight
materials with no shadows, ground plane, bump mapping or physical materials. Lite 2
keeps the original main geometry, materials and lighting; colour, auto-rotation and view reset live in
«Настроить» on the «Обзор» page. Three.js and all required assets are local.

The preview page uses explicit demo clock/LED data. This is not a hardware test.
